## Why

`PROJECT.md` names three governance offices and leaves their shape open:

> Both are **crate-or-convention as their nature dictates**, never named before their reaction exists.

That is the sentence a reader consults when asking whether one of the three should become a crate, and it
answers nothing. The self-law preamble every agent loads carries the same emptiness in the file with the widest
reach: *"三司 administer"* — three names, no referent for any, which is what makes them read as brandable.

## What Changes

- `PROJECT.md` replaces the open question with **why the question does not arise**: a crate is born when a
  dimension is built, because a dimension adds a new drift type and needs a boundary the self-law can react to
  — every dimension's `restrict_dependencies_to` naming no sibling **is** that reaction. A 司 adds no drift
  type; it surfaces, records or amends what a 儀 already found.
- The self-law preamble names each surface by **what it does** rather than as a set.
- `self-law-projection`'s requirement, which prescribes the preamble's content, states that rule together with
  the fact that nothing observes it.

## Two earlier attempts, and the class they share

Recorded so the next author does not spend the same rounds.

1. **A retirement of 校讎**, on the ground that it had "no code, no capability and no reaction". Two thirds
   false: `.github/CODEOWNERS` calls itself the amendment reaction, shipped source names the routing, and
   `self-law-projection`'s own spec governs "Layer 3 … governed by `CODEOWNERS`". The grep behind the premise
   swept `crates/`, `docs/` and the self-law projection, and never swept `.github/`.
2. **A location table** naming where each of the three lives. Review falsified three of three: `louke` consumes
   no baseline at all while the text claimed "every crate above it"; the 校讎 bullet cited the demo
   constitution, which `CODEOWNERS` says "gates nothing on its own", and omitted the file it calls primary; and
   `guibiao` was credited with a constitution document that is assembled in `tianheng`'s own projection
   subsystem. Its central argument — *spans crates ⇒ cannot be a crate* — also proves too much: `xingbiao`
   exists **because** its concern spans the dimensions, and it is a crate.

**Both attempts failed the same way: asserting a location or an absence without sweeping for it.** This change
makes no location claim. What answers the reader's question is the drift law, which is already stated two
sentences earlier and needs no sweep to be true.
